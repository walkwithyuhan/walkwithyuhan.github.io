A LITTLE ABOUT US

Hey, I’m Yuhan.

A girl from Sichuan, China, living in beautiful Singapore. I love cooking, taking walks with a podcast on—or off, while debating weird topics with my dear husband—and taking naps.

This little corner of the internet began with a manga I made using AI for my grandma, who sadly passed away in October 2026. I wanted a home for that original project, made for her. Then more weird little thoughts started popping up, and I kept making.

Whoever you are, thank you for visiting. I’m glad you’re here!
